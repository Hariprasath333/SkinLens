ABCDE self-check

Five warning signs that a mole might be a melanoma. Check your moles every few months in good light, with a mirror or someone to help for your back. One sign does not mean you have cancer, but it is a reason to show the mole to a doctor.

A Asymmetry

If you draw a line through the middle, the two halves do not match.

B Border

The edge is ragged, notched or blurred instead of smooth.

C Colour

Several colours or shades in one spot: brown, black, sometimes red, white or blue-grey.

D Diameter

Larger than 6 mm across (about the size of a pencil eraser). Melanomas can be smaller, so the other signs still count.

E Evolving

The spot is changing: it grows, changes shape or colour, or starts to itch, crust or bleed. This is the most important sign.

Also look out for

  • The "ugly duckling": a mole that looks different from all your other moles.
  • A new mole in adulthood, or a sore that does not heal.

See a doctor if you notice any of these signs. It is quick to check, and melanoma is very treatable when found early.
